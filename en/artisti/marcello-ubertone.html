The biography

Marcello Ubertone is a singer-songwriter who tells stories. Imagination and real life mingle in his songs. He has released the album «Meconio», created «Il concerto più piccolo del mondo» (The smallest concert in the world), and in his latest song-theatre show he gives voice to the characters of the Odyssey.
