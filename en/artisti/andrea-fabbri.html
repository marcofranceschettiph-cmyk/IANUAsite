The biography

Andrea Fabbri is a tattoo artist and painter from Rovigo. His artistic training and deep knowledge of art history have always guided his work. In tattooing he translates culture, technique and a painter’s sensibility into personal works, where every mark becomes expression and story.

Tattoo and painting are the two languages through which he expresses what he feels and what he sees. For him, art history is an inexhaustible source of inspiration: he loves Caravaggio for his truth, his light and that ability to turn shadow into emotion.
