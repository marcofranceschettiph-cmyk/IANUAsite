Biography

My name is Nihil. I was born on a date yet to be decided, on a distant planet (perhaps): Nihil Land. I am heir to the throne of a kingdom without land, which Father left me as my inheritance; a devastated planet where I lived alone for several aeons.

After a golden age, when milk and honey flowed and God and Satan walked hand in hand, a war emptied my land. The best of us left for other worlds. Not I, a wretched monarch without a crown. It was then, in the most absolute solitude, that I came to know you earthlings and your culture. It was thanks to a telepathic bond with Dente d’oro (Gold Tooth), the mighty pirate who plundered all my possessions and stole my soul. Ever since, I have been travelling to find it again.

I have stopped here, where you see me, in the right place to find myself again: a garden in bloom. I discovered music; on Nihil Land it did not exist. I find it an extraordinary vehicle for journeys of the mind, the kind that bring you in touch with your soul.
