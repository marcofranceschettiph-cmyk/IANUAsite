The biography

Marco Franceschetti (Ferrara, 1992) runs the operational side of IANUA: research, strategy, organisation. It is the work that supports the collective’s ideas.

He holds a degree in Economics and a master’s in Economics, Markets and Management from the University of Ferrara, and also studied at the Universidade da Coruña, in Galicia. From his studies and his work as an entrepreneur he brings to IANUA a way of looking at projects: direction, development, connections and execution.

He comes from singing and has been pursuing photography since the age of fourteen: today, that keen eye for the image is what leads him to take care of every detail in everything he devotes himself to.
