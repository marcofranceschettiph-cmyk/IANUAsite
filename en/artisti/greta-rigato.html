The biography

Greta Rigato is a performing arts professional whose multidisciplinary path weaves together dance, music, theatre, art and a humanities education.

She graduated in Modern Literature from the University of Padova, earned a diploma in piano at the Rovigo Conservatory and studied at the Accademia del Cinema e del Doppiaggio in Rome. She has studied classical and modern dance since childhood, later deepening contemporary dance in Italy and Germany. From 2012 to 2017 she danced with Duo Zappa-Mainolfi, touring South Africa, Germany and the United States; she then worked with the Münchner Philharmoniker.

She has been teaching since 2001 (Städtische Sing- und Musikschule in Munich; today the Musikschulen of Merano and Bolzano) and is co-programme director, accompanist and teacher at the International Academy of Music and the Summit Music Festival in New York. A self-taught draughtswoman and painter, she leads drawing and painting workshops for the IANUA-La Porta Dell'Arte collective, whose shows she takes part in as actress, pianist and dancer.
