The biography

Chiara Tosti (Umbertide, 1994) is a kinesiologist, dance movement therapist (APID) and dancer. Her artistic research develops at the intersection of contemporary dance, theatre and pedagogical and therapeutic practices, with constant attention to the language of the body as an expressive and relational tool.

She first trained at the CODARTS dance academy in the Netherlands (2013) and then took part in the Agora Coaching Project in Reggio Emilia (2015–2017), an advanced training programme directed by Michele Merola and Enrico Morelli, working with choreographers and dancers of international standing. In 2022 she earned a bachelor’s degree in Motor, Sport and Health Sciences from the University of Urbino and a Master’s in Pedagogy of Bodily Mediation and Symbolic-Anthropological Dance Movement Therapy from the Scuola di Pedagogia della Corporeità in Perugia. She also holds the National Level I Contemporary Dance Teacher Diploma (AICS).

Since 2018 she has worked with Compagnia Fabula Saltica of Rovigo, directed by Claudio Ronda, performing in Italy and abroad. In 2023 she made her debut as author and dancer with her first personal choreographic project, Io sono di legno, setting out on a path of independent creation. In 2021 she danced in two productions by and with Paolo Rosini, taking part in artistic residencies including Anghiari Dance Hub, and joined research projects such as C.R.E.A. Perugia (2022–2023), dancing works by Erion Kruja, Melania Olcina and Germán Jauregui.

In 2024 she performed in La Traviata with the Coro Lirico dell’Umbria and as a mime in Madama Butterfly at the Teatro Sociale in Rovigo, directed by Filippo Tonon. Since 2025 she has worked as an actress with Teatro del Lemming, directed by Massimo Munaro, after attending the advanced training course I cinque sensi dell'attore (The actor’s five senses).

In November 2025 she gave the national premiere of Eudemonia, a Fabula Saltica production: a work that weaves together contemporary dance and puppet theatre, extending her research towards the meeting of different performance languages. She currently works freelance, engaging with the contemporary scene and pursuing research oriented towards multidisciplinarity and the cross-pollination of the arts.
