Biography

In my music I explore the relationship between the real and the surreal. I start from concrete elements such as places, people and objects, which I portray musically with acoustic and traditional instruments, and I tell their subjective extensions – imaginary, dreamlike and emotional – which I portray instead through electronics and sound design.
