The vision

The project brings different arts together to tell the story of humanity through headdresses and costumes from all over the world: objects that recount their own lives through original stories, sounds, music and movement.

The private collection Da ogni capo del mondo, together with the artists of IANUA, becomes the starting point for a sensory journey through cultures, stories and traditions. Each piece on display evokes emotions, lives and identities, inviting the audience to reflect and imagine.

An immersive, personal experience, where every object tells a story and every story leaves a mark.

The Da ogni capo del mondo collection.

The collection

Da ogni capo del mondo is a private collection of headdresses, tribal and ethnic costumes, period clothing and accessories from every continent. The fruit of more than thirty years of passion, research and study, it now holds over 200 pieces of historical, cultural and anthropological significance.

The name plays on the double meaning of the Italian word capo: the head, on which the headdress rests, the heart of the collection, and the place, pointing to the many geographical origins of the pieces. A title that brings body and world together in a single cultural vision.

The aim

To create immersive, striking and truly original events, tailored around headdresses and garments from different parts of the world. Each creation is an invitation to listen, to discover, to meet.

Immersing ourselves in distant cultures is a powerful way to rediscover what makes us human: recognising differences, understanding them, and letting ourselves be surprised by the deep similarities that unite us. Music, new stories and dance weave together into a universal language.

The event can accompany a temporary exhibition of the collection, with guided tours by the curator and the collector, adaptable to theatres and exhibition spaces.

“Storytelling, made of voice, body and sound, is an eternal fire around which humanity gathers, today as always.”
