The poetry of chamber music

A journey in which music and poetry pass through each other. The word opens a threshold; the sound walks through it.

The programme sets works by Fauré, Sibelius, Grieg, Piazzolla, Elgar and Morricone alongside three original compositions by Nicola Elias Rigato, with trio transcriptions of orchestral pieces.

The verse of Rigato, Muschitiello and Taverna traces a path towards contemplation. Through the dialogue between music and poetry, the show offers a space to recognise ourselves in our emotions and to rediscover the beauty of listening to the chamber repertoire, between intimacy and power, shedding light on the shadows of human experience too.

“There is no shadow, only too little light”

The performers

Trio Ajar brings together three classically trained musicians; the narrator gives the verse a body.

Nicola Elias Rigato, piano and composition. A pianist and composer who graduated with honours and a special mention, he is published by Universal Edition. Winner of the Don Sante Montanaro International Prize, he has worked with RAI5 and the Sanremo Symphony Orchestra. His works have been recorded by Da Vinci Records. He is the founder of We-Choir and teaches at the Four Piano Academy.

Luca Talassi, cello. Trained with Marianne Chen and Luigi Puxeddu, he has won national competitions, including the Premio Alberghini. Since 2023 he has played with the Orchestra Giovanile Luigi Cherubini, founded by Riccardo Muti, and he has passed the orchestral audition for I Pomeriggi Musicali in Milan.

Domenico Nicola Percetti, violin. A graduate in Violin and Chamber Music with honours and a special mention, he stands out in modern and Baroque repertoire. A prizewinner at the “Tartini ad Ancona” International Competition, he works with Frau Musika and has been first violin and concertmaster of the Orchestra Barocca delle Marche.

Alessandro Alfonsi, narrator. A musician, actor and composer, he holds a degree in Theatre from DAMS and a diploma in Jazz. He trained with Vasco Mirandola, Ewa Benesz and Enrico Bonavera. Founder and artistic director of Sonic Poets, he combines music and acting in original productions. With the Psycodrummers he has worked with Giorgia, Achille Lauro and Levante, and has also performed on X Factor.
