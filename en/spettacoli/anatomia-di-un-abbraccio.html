The show

Dancer and pianist share a stage of joint creation, where the constraint, and at the same time the potential, is expressed by an embrace.

At times the dancer’s movement follows the sound; at others, it hinders it. In turn, the music follows the spaces left empty and open by the dancer’s contact movements. This is why some compositions are reworked and adapted live, creating a living score that is always open to change.

The two arts meet through a system of constraints and possibilities born of contact, weight, friction and balance.

Anatomia di un abbraccio is an exploration of relationship: of how far we can be both limit and possibility for one another, and of how, in an encounter, we can make room until we almost “vanish” into the other’s space.

Photographs: IANUA, 2026.

Where it began

Anatomia di un abbraccio takes its name and its starting point from the work by Luna Lu, in which two hearts, freed from their ribcages, meet in an intimate, suspended embrace.

From this image grew the research of Chiara Tosti and Nicola Elias Rigato: how far can we be constraint and possibility for one another? How much can the body change the music, and how much can the music change the body?

The dance enters the score, moves through it, interrupts it and lets itself be crossed by it. Some pieces are adapted and transformed through contact, while Chiara’s movement lets itself be guided, supported and shaped by Nicola’s sound.

The encounter thus becomes a practice of mutual listening: making room for the other without losing oneself.

In this shared space, the boundary between I and you, body and sound, constraint and possibility grows ever thinner. The other’s threshold becomes a place to find oneself again.

“Life is the art of encounter.”
