A happiness that goes beyond

Eudaimonìa, from the Greek εὐδαιμονία: from εὖ, “good”, and δαίμων, “demon; fate”. An ancient Greek word for a kind of happiness that goes beyond simple pleasure or momentary satisfaction: a fulfilled, authentic life, reached by realising one’s potential and pursuing virtue.

But what happens when our inner demon stands between us and the possibility of being happy?

Eudemonìa is a physical and emotional journey exploring the body, identity, the double, the reflection, truth and lies. A piece that blends contemporary dance and figure theatre to tell a story both intimate and universal.

Photographs: Marina Carluccio.

Who is manipulating whom?

The dancer’s body and the puppet’s merge, chase and challenge each other, in a constant tension between control and submission. As the author writes in her diary: “It’s as if I had two personalities… I feel like a puppet in its hands.”

The puppet, made especially by Mariella Carbone, becomes both a stage device and a therapeutic metaphor: it can be manipulated or manipulating, victim or tormentor.

Eudemonìa becomes an act of resilience and truth. The story of a mind that splits in two, of a body that does not recognise itself, of a soul looking for its own voice.

“We can make peace with our demons. We can come back to life.”

The artists

A piece born from the collaboration between Compagnia Fabula Saltica and the IANUA collective, dedicated to Paola Bianchini.

Chiara Tosti, author and performer. Freelance dancer, kinesiologist, dance movement therapist (APID) and co-founder of the IANUA collective. She trained at CODARTS in the Netherlands and with the Agora Coaching Project in Reggio Emilia; she holds a degree in Sport Science and a Master’s in the Pedagogy of Bodily Mediation and Dance Movement Therapy. She has worked with Fabula Saltica since 2018, and with Teatro del Lemming and Paolo Rosini. In November 2025 she made her debut as author and performer with Eudemonìa.

Mariella Carbone, puppet. Architect, art therapist and puppet maker, she moves between architecture, art therapy and figure theatre. An illustrator and set designer, she creates masks, puppets, dolls, figures and sculptures for collectors, companies and festivals in Italy and abroad.

Nicola Elias Rigato, music. Pianist and composer, founder of the IANUA collective. He graduated with honours and is published by Universal Edition; winner of the Don Sante Montanaro International Prize, he has worked with RAI 5 and the Sanremo Symphony Orchestra.
