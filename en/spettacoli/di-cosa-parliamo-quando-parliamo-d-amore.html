An evening to listen again

In an intimate atmosphere, the voice of actor Alessandro Alfonsi moves through poems and literary texts devoted to love.

Nicola Elias Rigato’s piano converses with the words through the great Romantic repertoire, film scores and jazz, weaving the pieces together with improvisation, quotations and crossings between genres.

Three short listening guides lead the audience into the mechanisms that make music able to move us, turning the show into an accessible and engaging experience even for those with no musical training.

Desire, wonder, knowledge, unity, eros, care and the search for oneself: these are the stages of the literary journey. An evening to listen again, and to listen to ourselves.

Photographs: IANUA.

The texts

Texts by Plato, Jalal al-Din Rumi, Giacomo Leopardi, Dante, Chandra Livia Candiani, Andrea Marcolongo, Wystan Hugh Auden, Rainer Maria Rilke, Massimo Recalcati, Alessandro Baricco and Nicola Elias Rigato.

The performers

Two artists of the collective on the same stage: the word and the piano.

Alessandro Alfonsi, narrator. Actor, musician, composer and educator, his work grows from the meeting of word, body and music: sound does not accompany the scene, it becomes part of it. Trained at the Venezze Conservatory, he has worked with Teatro del Lemming; he is a co-founder of the Psycodrummers, with whom he has performed alongside Giorgia, Achille Lauro and Levante, and the creator of Sonic Poets.

Nicola Elias Rigato, piano and arrangements. Pianist, composer and music researcher, he graduated with honours in Piano, Composition and Chamber Music; he is published by Universal Edition and has released four albums of original music. Founder of the IANUA collective, he is the author of the piano method La Mano, La Mente, Il Cuore.
