Who processes the data

IANUA is an artistic collective based in Rovigo, currently in the process of being established as an association.

Until the association has been established, the data controller is Nicola Rigato, acting on behalf of the collective. For questions or requests about your data, write to ianuaeventi@gmail.com.

Once the association has been established, we will update this page with its details.

What data and why

When you write to us through the form

You give us your name, email address and message, and we use them only to reply to you. We delete them within 12 months of our last exchange, unless a collaboration has begun that requires us to keep them.

When you write to us by email, phone or Instagram

We use the data you give us to reply to you, with the same retention periods as for the form. Our email runs on Gmail, so messages also pass through Google’s servers.

If you take part as an artist

Through the call form we collect the data needed to assess your application and, if you take part, to organise your participation in PRO-FILI Festival: name, contact details, presentation, images of your work.

We publish your name, biography and photos on the website only if you give your consent in the form. After the festival, your page remains online in the past events archive, linked to the edition you took part in.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to ianuaeventi@gmail.com: within 30 days we remove all references to you from the website, namely your name, photos, biography and personal page. Anything already published before that remains lawful.

The form is managed with Google Forms, so the data is also stored by Google. Applications that are not selected are deleted at the end of the festival edition.

If you sign up for a workshop

We use your registration data to organise the workshop and to contact you. For minors, we ask for the parents’ details and consent. We publish workshop photos only with the consent of the people portrayed and, for minors, with the consent of both parents. Photos remain in the past events archive unless someone writes to us asking for specific content to be removed: we do so within 30 days. We never write a minor’s name next to their photo.

Cookies

The website does not use cookies or any other tools that store information on your device, and it does not use analytics or tracking systems. That is why you will not find any cookie banner.

Fonts are loaded from the website itself. The “Add to calendar” button creates the event file directly in your browser. Links to Google Maps, Instagram and Google Forms lead to external websites, which follow their own privacy rules.

Who receives the data

We do not sell or transfer your data. It is processed on our behalf only by the services we use for the website and for contacts:

  • Google (Gmail and Google Forms), for email and for the application and registration forms.
  • GitHub (GitHub Pages), which hosts the website: for security purposes it logs the IP address of visitors.

Google and GitHub may also process data in the United States, with the safeguards of the EU-US Data Privacy Framework: their own privacy policies apply (Google, GitHub).

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to access the data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict its use or to receive it in a readable format. You can also object to processing based on our legitimate interest and withdraw any consent you have given us.

Write to ianuaeventi@gmail.com: we will reply within one month. If you believe your data is being processed improperly, you can lodge a complaint with the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (the Italian Data Protection Authority).

We do not make automated decisions about people and we do not create profiles.
