IANUA · Artistic collective · Rovigo

The collective · La Porta Dell'Arte

Connecting arts

ianua, from the Latin: door. A collective of artists working together, connecting their visions and discoveries.

Music, dance, theatre, word and visual arts within the same project: not placed side by side on a programme, but the fruit of a dialogue on the creative process, in search of authenticity.

IANUA was born in 2025 from a simple observation: an artist working alone spends more time organising than creating. Together, the effort of one becomes the advantage of many, connecting creation, experience, understanding, sharing, education and the growth of transformative communities.

Art is a door to an inner world, a bridge towards individual and collective metamorphoses. Events, in turn, are a door to a shared language. Alongside the shows, the collective runs regular creative workshops that teach the exercise of creativity. After all, a door can be crossed in both directions.

IANUA (door, in Latin) is an interdisciplinary collective and cultural project that develops artistic productions, events, workshops and local projects, bringing music, theatre, poetry, visual arts and philosophy into dialogue.

IANUA grew out of meeting and working with artists of national standing, and has built its own production activity through seven original shows. Among them, Vedo un suono che si leva, with Muschitiello, gave rise to a new musical production published by Universal Edition and reviewed by the magazine La Decima Sinfonia; L'Orlando Ritrovato was hosted in 2026 by the International Academy of Music (IAM) in Castelnuovo di Garfagnana.

In 2025 IANUA was a partner of the Festival Briciole in Modena, taking responsibility for artistic direction and running workshops and events. It has also worked in urban regeneration and cultural participation through projects such as Edicò, with workshops in urban philosophy, collective drawing, dialogue and performative practices.

In this way IANUA develops a model of integrated cultural production, able to connect artistic quality, education, innovation and the enhancement of the local area.

Three lines of work

A show and a workshop

Every creation has a show to bring to the stage and a workshop that opens up its language, offering it to understanding and direct experience. That is why our work doesn’t end when the lights go down.

1

Musicsound as storytelling

Concerts, lecture-concerts and original compositions, where music works together with poetry, visual arts and the stage. Classical repertoire and contemporary music, often previously unheard.

Productions
Vedo un suono che si leva (Universal Edition) · Luci ed Ombre nella poesia della musica · L'Orlando Ritrovato
Workshop
Guided listening and composition, also for those with no musical training.
2

Bodymovement as language

Contemporary dance and performances built around the space that hosts them: a cloister, a fish market, a villa are not interchangeable backdrops.

Productions
Anatomia di un abbraccio, for pianist and dancer
Workshop
Expressive body work for those who have never danced, with visual arts and live music.
3

Worddialogue as practice

Theatre, staged readings and dialogue meetings. The line that brings the audience inside the work instead of leaving it in the stalls.

Productions
Di cosa parliamo quando parliamo d'amore · Nihil Land
Format
PORTAmi, monthly dialogue meetings · Dialogue groups
Workshop
Theatre and drama therapy, with experience in disability and early school leaving.

Daniele De Plano

Director · author · known as NIHIL

Born in 1964, he was frontman of the Tuscan new wave band Nomenclatura in the 1980s (Contempo Record) and graduated in History and Criticism of Cinema from the University of Pisa.

Director at the Festival Puccini in Torre del Lago (Turandot 1998, Manon Lescaut 2003, up to 2025) and artistic director of the Festival from 2014 to 2016. For Rai he produced more than fifty episodes of Prima della Prima and created the formats Lezioni di Suono and Migrazioni; he has worked with Sciarrino, Battistelli, Fedele and Marco Angius.

Director of short films (Il regalo di Natale, nominated for the David di Donatello) and of the feature film Ristabbanna with Ben Gazzara (Shanghai International Film Festival; best film at the Rome Independent Film Festival). A docu-fiction on Igor Mitoraj is in preparation. With the Nihil Land project he is NIHIL.
