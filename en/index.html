The collective

A collective of artists working together, connecting their visions and discoveries. Music, dance, theatre, word and visual arts within the same project: not placed side by side on a programme, but the fruit of a dialogue on the creative process, in search of authenticity.

Art is a door to an inner world. After all, a door can be crossed in both directions.IANUA, September 2026

IANUA was born in 2025 from a simple observation: an artist working alone spends more time organising than creating. Together, the effort of one becomes the advantage of many, connecting creation, experience, understanding, sharing, education and the growth of transformative communities.

The collective grew out of meeting and working with artists of national standing, and has built its own production activity through seven original shows.

Among them, Vedo un suono che si leva, with Muschitiello, gave rise to a new musical production published by Universal Edition and reviewed by the magazine La Decima Sinfonia; L'Orlando Ritrovato was hosted in 2026 by the International Academy of Music in Castelnuovo di Garfagnana.

In 2025 IANUA was a partner of the Festival Briciole in Modena, taking responsibility for artistic direction and running workshops and events. It has also worked in urban regeneration and cultural participation through projects such as Edicò, with workshops in urban philosophy, collective drawing, dialogue and performative practices.

In this way IANUA develops a model of integrated cultural production, able to connect artistic quality, education, innovation and the enhancement of the local area.

Productions

The shows

Live music, dance, word and image. Each production is born from an encounter between languages, and from the artists who move through it.

01 / 05

Anatomia di un abbraccio

2026 · Pianist and dancer · 40'

Dancer and pianist share a stage of joint creation, in which the constraint, and with it the potential, is expressed by an embrace.

The two arts meet through a system of constraints and possibilities generated by contact, weight, friction and balance. A study of relationship: of how far we can be both limit and possibility for one another.

About the show

02 / 05

Nihil Land

Live concept album · 13 tracks · also for voice and piano

Nihil, before he was Nihil, crossed a thousand worlds and lingered long on each of them, leaving traces of himself.

Heir to the throne of a kingdom without land, the alien Nihil reaches our planet after long cosmic wanderings, and here he discovers music. Nihil Land speaks to us and about us, and shows us, through an alien’s eyes, the complexity of being human.

About the show

03 / 05

Vedo un suono che si leva

Music and poetry · baritenor and piano · 70'

Is it possible to see music? Yes: through the poetic word, the evocation of images, and listening guides by a composer.

Ten songs by Nicola Elias Rigato on poems by Nicola Muschitiello, published by Universal Edition. In concert the poet reads his verses and brings them to life; the word becomes song in the voice of baritenor Damiano Lombardo.

About the show

04 / 05

Luci ed Ombre

Music and poetry · trio and narrator

A chamber journey between word and sound, between light and darkness, between memory and transformation.

Trio Ajar sets Fauré, Sibelius, Grieg, Piazzolla, Elgar and Morricone alongside three original compositions by Nicola Elias Rigato. The verses, in the voice of Alessandro Alfonsi, trace a path towards contemplation.

About the show

05 / 05

Di cosa parliamo quando parliamo d'amore

Music, poetry and theatre · piano and narrator · 60'

An immersive show: a moment of listening and reflection around love.

Nicola Elias Rigato’s piano converses with the voice of Alessandro Alfonsi across the Romantic repertoire, film scores and jazz, with three short listening guides. An evening to listen again, and to listen to ourselves.

About the show
