The idea

The IANUA artistic collective presented PRO-FILI, Edition 0, part of the municipal Ottobre Rodigino programme and conceived as a short choral journey in which the audience does not simply watch the events but enters into a direct relationship with local artists.

The thread becomes a conceptual engine and a way of drawing emerging artists closer to their possible audience of tomorrow, and the audience closer to kinds of art that may still be unknown to them.

The soul of PRO-FILI

  • WorkshopsImmersive workshops for all ages, open to the public.
  • Local artistsShowcasing artists from the Rovigo area and their current work.
  • GuestsFour special guests alongside the local artists: painter Massimo Marchioro, singer-songwriter FILO, visual artist CAINO and musician NIHIL.
  • The thread as a networkA moment of direct, informal encounter between citizens and artists, strengthening the network between local cultural organisations, media and the public.
  • ProgrammeA varied programme: workshops, music, photography, installation, dialogue.
  • Art and responsibilityReal relationships between people, in which art is a bond and a responsibility.
